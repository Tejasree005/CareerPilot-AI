ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Career planning, made clearer.

CareerPilot AI is a student portfolio project demonstrating a Python-powered career exploration workflow.

01

Discover

Search and filter sample opportunities by keywords, type, and location.

02

Prepare

Compare resume text against job requirements with a transparent keyword-overlap method.

03

Organize

Save sample jobs and record demo applications in a local SQLite database.

Project transparency: job listings are illustrative sample data, the analyzer is a basic keyword tool rather than a trained AI model, and application submissions are stored locally only.
